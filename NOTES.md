# Personal Notes

## Approach

I started by personally reviewing and taking handwritten notes on the project, noting requirements and constraints. I then added a hand-drawn workflow wireframe for my own visualization. When it came to building, I started by planning the error handling so that testing could be quickly but efficiently handled within the 4-hour project period.

---

## Future Considerations

The MVP of this automation is currently functional, but there are always further considerations to make. Some that I have in mind already are:

- Edge cases, in the case some reports need to be updated for some reason.
- End of year reporting (aggregated).
- Further security reviews to consider tightening of scopes where possible.
- Updating departments and categories to match AUTO1 branding and org structure.
- Removing a full row when validation errors occur; this isn't ideal considering UX, but it is a small nuisance at present and can be improved as needed.
- Considerations of where new sheets (tabs) are created for reporting. Currently they are newly created to the right of whichever sheet you have actively open while running the tests.
- Adding a note that when sending the calendar invite to oneself in testing, you don't receive an email for the invite, but when sending it to anyone else, they would receive one.
